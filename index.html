How it fits together

          
Three modules. One set of rails.

          
Each module does one job well. Underneath, they share identity, settlement, indexing and verification — so you sign in once and every result is checkable the same way.

        


        

          

            
          


          

            
L1
Identity
Sign in with X (read-only scope) and link up to three wallets with one signature each.

            
L2
Settlement
USDG on Robinhood Chain for swaps, auctions and bounties. Exact approvals only.

            
L3
Canonical indexer
Reads verified on-chain events for feeds, badges and pool state. Never holds funds.

            
L4
Verification
Open-source verifier, attestation checked against vendor services, hashes checked in your browser.

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          
Safety

          
Your keys. Your range. Your signature.

        

        

          
01No admin key moves funds
Treasury and upgrades sit behind a Safe and a timelock, with per-pool caps.

          
02Exact approvals
Every deposit approves the amount you typed — never unlimited.

          
03Nothing auto-executes
Tailing and quotes only prefill. You review, size and sign every transaction.

          
04Limits live on-chain
Fees, flow caps and closure budgets are enforced by the hook and cannot be bypassed.

We read your handle, name and avatar. We cannot post, like or DM.

          
06Paid on any verdict
Proof bounties pay the same whatever the result, so nobody is paid to say yes.

FAQ

          
Questions, answered.

          
The short version of what RETRA is and how it treats your funds.

        

        

          

            What is RETRA?
            
One app on Robinhood Chain with three modules: Markets for stock-token pools, Callouts for sharing and copying LP positions, and Proof for paying to re-run research. You sign in once and use all three.

          

          

            Do I need the $RETRA token to use it?
            
No. Trading, tailing and reading certificates work with any wallet. Holding $RETRA only unlocks tiers such as lower bounty fees.

          

          

            Does tailing a callout copy the caller's profit?
            
No. Tailing copies a range, not a result. You choose the size, review the transaction and sign it yourself, and LP positions can lose value.

          

          

            Why do Markets fees change during the day?
            
Liquidity is thinner outside regular exchange hours, so fees rise and flow limits tighten to protect LPs. Regular hours are the cheapest at 0.05%.

          

          

            What happens if a paper fails replication?
            
The certificate shows the paper's value next to the re-run value and marks the claim Failed. It means the result was not reproduced with the published code and data, not that anyone cheated. The bounty pays the same either way.

          

          

            Can RETRA move my funds or post on my X account?
            
No. There is no admin key that moves user funds, every approval is for the exact amount, and X access is read-only.
